Legal translation

Legal translation your counsel can review.

We translate legal documents. We do not give legal advice and we do not replace your lawyer. The wording stays close to the source.

Why it helps

  • Close translation, not a rewrite
  • Defined terms kept consistent
  • An NDA before confidential papers are sent

What is included

  • Contracts
  • Policies and terms
  • Court and registry papers
  • Legal correspondence
  • Certified translations for UK courts

How it works

  1. Counsel

    Tell us who must review the result.

  2. Terms

    We list the defined terms.

  3. Translate

    Translation and editing.

  4. Return

    Queries are listed, not hidden.

Who it is for

Law firms, in-house legal teams and companies with cross-border papers.

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No.

Do you certify the translation?

Yes, for use in the UK. If an authority abroad requires a sworn translation, we arrange it through a partner.
