A note on two of them

German (Switzerland): written Swiss German follows Swiss Standard German. It uses ss instead of ß and has its own vocabulary. Spoken Swiss German dialect is covered for subtitles and transcription.

Cantonese: for subtitles and transcription of spoken Cantonese, and for Traditional Chinese written for Hong Kong readers.
