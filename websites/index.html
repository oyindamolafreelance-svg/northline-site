Websites

Websites that explain the offer and hold up.

We design and build business sites, online shops and landing pages. A site is finished when it is clear, fast and ready to hand over, not when it only looks finished.

Why it helps

  • A structure built around what you sell
  • A design your team can update
  • Speed and accessibility checked before launch

What is included

  • Business and marketing websites
  • Online shops (Shopify or WooCommerce)
  • Landing pages
  • Redesigns and moves from Wix or Squarespace
  • CMS setup and training
  • Domain, hosting and business email setup
  • Page copy in English, and in other languages

Packages

  • One-page siteOne page with your offer, contact form and map. For a launch or a sole trader.
  • Business siteFive to eight pages, a blog if you want one, and local SEO setup.
  • Online shopProducts, payments, delivery rules and order emails.
  • Multilingual siteAny of the above in two or more languages, with hreflang and translated search terms.

Every package is quoted after a short brief, so you only pay for the pages you need.

How it works

  1. Brief

    We agree the audience, the offer and the pages that must exist.

  2. Design

    You review the layout before the build starts.

  3. Build

    We develop the site, the forms and the content model.

  4. Check

    Every launch is tested against the Core Web Vitals thresholds. You receive the report.

Who it is for

Companies and sole traders who need a site that sells a service, a product or a launch.

Questions

Can we edit the site ourselves?

Yes. CMS training is part of the handover. You own the site.

Do you redesign old sites?

Yes. We keep the pages that already rank, set up redirects, and replace what does not help the buyer.

Who owns the domain?

You do. We register it in your name or connect the one you have. See domains and business email.

How long does a site take?

It depends on the number of pages and how ready the content is. The quote gives a date.

Tell us what you need. You get a written quote before any work starts.
