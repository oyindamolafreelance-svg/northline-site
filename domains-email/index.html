Domains and business email

Your domain and your inbox, set up properly.

We register your domain in your name, set up business email such as you@yourcompany.com, and configure the DNS records that keep your mail out of spam folders.

Why it helps

  • The domain is registered to you, not to us
  • Mail from your domain is authenticated, so it lands in the inbox
  • Website, email and DNS work together from day one

What is included

  • Domain registration and renewal reminders (.com, .co.uk, .de and more)
  • Business email on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC records
  • Moving existing mailboxes and contacts
  • Shared addresses such as info@ and quotes@
  • Connecting the domain to your website, with HTTPS
  • Email signatures

How it works

  1. Check

    We look at what you have: domain, registrar, current email and website.

  2. Register

    We register or transfer the domain in your name.

  3. Set up

    Mailboxes, DNS records and the website connection.

  4. Test

    We send test mail to Gmail and Outlook and confirm it is authenticated.

Who it is for

New businesses, sole traders, and firms still using a personal Gmail or Hotmail address for work.

Questions

Who pays for Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

You pay the provider directly, on your own account. We set it up and hand over the admin login.

Why does my email go to spam?

Usually because SPF, DKIM or DMARC is missing or wrong. Since 2024 Gmail and Yahoo have required these records from bulk senders, and they help every sender.

Can you move us from another email provider?

Yes. We move mailboxes and contacts and plan the switch so no mail is lost.

Do you hold our domain?

No. The domain is registered to you. We only ask for access while we work.
