SEO

Search that matches the page you ship.

We set up technical SEO, on-page SEO, local SEO and multilingual SEO. Content is written for what people search, not added at the end.

Why it helps

  • A site search engines can read
  • Language versions with hreflang and locale URLs
  • Advice you can check yourself in Search Console

What is included

  • Technical SEO
  • On-page SEO
  • Local SEO, including your Google Business Profile
  • Multilingual SEO with hreflang and locale URLs
  • Keyword research in each target language
  • Content writing for SEO
  • Conversion work on key pages

How it works

  1. Audit

    We read the current site, the search terms and the list of markets.

  2. Plan

    You approve the pages and the language structure.

  3. Implement

    Titles, links, speed issues and locale tags are fixed in the build.

  4. Review

    We show what changed. Rankings are never guaranteed.

Who it is for

Businesses that need to be found in more than one city or more than one language.

Questions

Do you guarantee a ranking?

No. We fix what we control and we report on it. Nobody can sell you a position.

Is multilingual SEO just translation?

No. Each language needs its own search terms, titles and URLs.

Do you set up Google Business Profile?

Yes, along with Bing Places and Apple Business Connect if you want them.
