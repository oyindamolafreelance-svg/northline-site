MTPE and quality checks

A human check, at the level the text needs.

Some texts need a full translation, edit and proofread. Some need a careful post-edit of machine output. We name the level before we start, and we do not pretend they are the same job.

Why it helps

  • The level of review is written down
  • Glossaries and translation memories stay with your account
  • Errors are classed, not waved through

What is included

  • Translation, editing and proofreading
  • Machine translation post-editing (MTPE)
  • Linguistic quality assurance (LQA) with an error report
  • Localisation testing
  • Glossary and style guide creation
  • Translation memory and terminology management

How it works

  1. Level

    We agree full human translation or post-editing.

  2. Assets

    Glossary, memory and style notes.

  3. Work

    The passes you ordered, and no extra pass you did not.

  4. Report

    On an LQA job, you get a short error report.

Who it is for

Agencies and product teams who already have machine output, or who need a formal quality check. To have an existing translation checked, see proofreading and revision.

Questions

Is post-editing cheaper?

Often, when the machine output is good enough. If it is not, we say so and quote a full translation.

Do you train public AI on our files?

No.
