machine translation post editing

A human check, at the level the text needs.

Some texts need a full translation, edit and proof. Some need a careful post edit of machine output. We name the level before we start. We do not pretend they are the same job.

Why it helps

  • The level of review is written down
  • Glossaries and translation memories stay on the account
  • Errors are classed, not waved through

What is included

  • Translation, editing and proofreading
  • Machine translation post editing
  • Linguistic quality assurance
  • Localisation testing
  • Glossary and style guide creation
  • Translation memory and terminology care

Process

  1. Level

    We agree full human translation or post editing.

  2. Assets

    Glossary, memory and style notes.

  3. Work

    The agreed passes, and no silent extra pass you did not order.

  4. Report

    On an LQA job, you get a short error report.

Who it is for

Agencies and product teams who already have machine output, or who need a formal quality check.

Questions

Is post editing cheaper?

Often, when the machine output is good enough. If it is not, we say so and quote a full translation.

Do you train public AI on our files?

No.
