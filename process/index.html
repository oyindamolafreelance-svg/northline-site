Process

How a job runs.

You see the work before it is called finished. Language jobs follow translation, editing and proofreading, then a final check, at the level you ordered.

  1. Scope

    We write down the job, the languages, the deadline and what is out of scope.

  2. Build or translate

    Design, engineering or the language passes. You can review along the way.

  3. Check

    A speed report for a launch. For language, a final read against the brief by a second person.

  4. Hand over

    Files, access and ownership. Ongoing care only if you want it.

Questions

What is the revision policy?

One round of revisions is included on the files we delivered, within the period stated in your quote. New source text is a new job.

What if you miss the date?

We tell you as soon as we see a risk, not on the day. The terms explain what happens if we miss an agreed date.

Tell us what you need. You get a written quote before any work starts.
