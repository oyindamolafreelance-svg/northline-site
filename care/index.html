Care plans

Care plans so the site keeps working.

A site needs updates, backups and someone watching that it is up. Care is a monthly plan. Hosting, domain and analytics setup can also be booked on their own.

Why it helps

  • Updates on a schedule
  • A named contact when something breaks
  • You still own the domain and the code

What is included

  • Website maintenance
  • Security updates
  • Backups
  • Uptime checks
  • Hosting and domain management
  • Analytics setup
  • Small content edits
  • Translation of new pages, if your site is multilingual

How it works

  1. Access

    We list what we need. Accounts stay in your name.

  2. Baseline

    We record the current speed, forms and backups.

  3. Routine

    Updates follow the plan you chose.

  4. Report

    You get a short note of what changed each month.

Who it is for

Owners who do not want to be the one who notices the contact form has stopped working.

Questions

Do you hold the domain?

No. The domain and the code stay in your name.

Can we cancel?

Yes. The notice period is written in your plan, and you keep everything when you leave.

Do you look after sites you did not build?

Yes, after a short check of how the site is built and hosted.

Tell us what you need. You get a written quote before any work starts.
