Medical translation

Medical translation for readers who cannot guess.

We translate medical and life sciences texts for companies that need accuracy and a careful process. An NDA is available before files are shared.

Why it helps

  • Terminology agreed before the bulk of the work
  • A second reader on the agreed scope
  • Confidential handling, with an NDA on request

What is included

  • Clinical and regulatory documents
  • Patient-facing text
  • Medical device instructions and leaflets
  • Life sciences marketing that must stay accurate
  • Glossary creation

How it works

  1. NDA

    Ask for an NDA before you send sensitive files.

  2. Terms

    We fix product names and units.

  3. Translate

    Translation, editing and a final check.

  4. Query

    Unclear source lines are queried, not guessed.

Who it is for

Medical, life sciences and device companies, and the agencies who serve them.

Questions

Do you give medical advice?

No. We translate. If your process requires it, a clinician must approve text that will be used with patients.

Is the work confidential?

Yes. Access is limited to the people on the job. We do not use your files to train a public AI model.
