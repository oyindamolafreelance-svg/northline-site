Pricing

How we price.

Every job gets a fixed written quote before work starts. Here is what each price is based on and what makes it go up or down.

ServicePriced byWhat changes the price
Certified translationPer document or pageAmount of text, language pair, deadline
Document translationPer wordLanguage pair, subject, file format and layout work, deadline
Proofreading and revisionPer 1,000 words or per hourProofreading or full revision, quality of the existing translation
Subtitling and transcriptionPer minute of video or audioLanguage pair, speed of speech, timing from scratch or from a template, SDH
Website and app localisationPer word, plus a fixed fee for checksNumber of languages, repeated text, CMS work, right-to-left layout
WebsiteFixed project priceNumber of pages, shop features, copywriting, number of languages
Domains and business emailFixed setup feeNumber of mailboxes, moving existing mail
SEOMonthlyNumber of pages, markets and languages
Care planMonthlySize of the site, update frequency, editing hours

Getting a price

Send the file, the URL or a short brief on the quote form. The quote states the price, the currency, the delivery date and how to pay. Nothing starts until you accept it.
