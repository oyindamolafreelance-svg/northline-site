Subtitling and transcription

Words on screen, timed and readable.

We subtitle, caption and transcribe audio and video. Subtitles follow the reading speed and line length in your style guide, or a standard we agree with you.

Why it helps

  • Reading speed kept in view
  • Speaker labels when you need them
  • Files you can import straight into your editor

What is included

  • Translated subtitles
  • Same-language captions
  • Subtitles for the deaf and hard of hearing (SDH)
  • Audio and video transcription, verbatim or clean
  • Spotting and timing
  • Burned-in subtitled video
  • Voice-over coordination through a partner
  • Audio and data annotation to your guidelines

File formats

SRT, WebVTT, EBU-STL, TTML/DFXP, SBV, and Word or Excel transcripts. Ask if your platform needs something else.

How it works

  1. Media

    Send the file or a link, and the language pair.

  2. Rules

    Character limits, reading speed, tone and whether it is verbatim.

  3. Time

    We spot the video, or translate your existing template.

  4. Watch

    A linguistic check against the picture once the cut is final.

Who it is for

Studios, training teams, YouTubers and product teams with video in more than one language.

Questions

How is it priced?

Per minute of video or audio. Language pair, speed of speech and turnaround change the price.

Do you interpret meetings?

No. Live interpreting is not offered.

Can you book a voice?

We coordinate it. The recording partner is named in the quote.

Tell us what you need. You get a written quote before any work starts.
