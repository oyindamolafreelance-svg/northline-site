Process

Four steps. Then it is yours.

A build is usually a few weeks. A localization quote starts from the URL or the file. Either way, the work is visible before it ships.

01 Scope

Write the job.

Audit the current site or brief the new one. Languages, speed budget, and what is out of scope.

02 Build

Make it visible.

Design, engineering, or adaptation. You review the work, not a status email.

03 Review

Check what ships.

Performance pass, and a human pass on every locale. Glossary locked before launch.

04 Launch

Hand it over.

Domain, repo, and CMS in your name. Care retainer only if you want the studio to stay.
