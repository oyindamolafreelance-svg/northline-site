patent translation services

Patent translation with the claims kept straight.

We translate patent texts for firms that care about consistent terms. Claims language is not smoothed into marketing.

Why it helps

  • Terms kept stable across a family of documents
  • Queries instead of silent changes
  • An NDA before the specification moves

What is included

  • Patent specifications you assign
  • Claims
  • Abstracts
  • Related technical correspondence in scope

Process

  1. Family

    Send related documents so terms stay stable.

  2. Lock

    We confirm the terms that must not move.

  3. Translate

    A specialist pass, then an edit.

  4. Check

    Numbers, references and claim order are checked.

Who it is for

Patent firms and in house teams filing or reading in another language.

Questions

Are you a patent attorney?

No. This is translation. Legal filing advice stays with your attorney.

Can you match an earlier translation?

Yes, if you share it. That is the point of a glossary and a translation memory.
