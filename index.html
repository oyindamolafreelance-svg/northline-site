How a project runs

  1. Brief

    We write down the job, the languages and the date.

  2. Make

    Design, build or translate, with you able to review.

  3. Check

    A speed report for a site, or a second linguist for a translation.

  4. Hand over

    You own the files. Ongoing care is optional.

Questions

Can one agency build and translate?

Yes. You can also hire only the build, or only the translation.

Which languages do you cover?

European and Asian languages, into and out of English. See the languages page. The quote names who will work on your language pair.

Do you provide certified translations?

Yes, for use in the UK. See certified translation.

Where are you based?

Glasgow, United Kingdom. Clients can be anywhere.

Do you interpret live calls or events?

No. We do written translation, subtitling and transcription only.
